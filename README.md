# Advanced Hospitality Data Architecture & Business Intelligence

Welcome to my hospitality data portfolio. This repository contains advanced architectural proposals and business intelligence tools designed to optimize revenue management, automate manual reporting, and unify data across mega-resort ecosystems.

### The Portfolio Projects

This repository is divided into two distinct, highly focused projects:

#### 1. [Project HARLTON](./Project%20HARLTON/proposal/proposal.md)
**Hospitality Asset & Revenue Learning, Tactical Optimization Network**
*   **Focus:** Strategic Revenue Optimization and Asset Management.
*   **Concept:** A modern architecture designed to replace arbitrary room upgrades and short-sighted group quoting with a system that balances short-term RevPAR against long-term Customer Lifetime Value (CLV).

#### 2. [Project H2](./Project%20H2/proposal.md)
**Unified Executive Business Intelligence & Automated Reporting**
*   **Focus:** Tactical Data Execution, SQL Architecture, and Automated BI.
*   **Concept:** A blueprint for end-to-end reporting tools. It outlines the creation of self-contained HTML executive dashboards backed by complex SQL stored procedures, designed to replace manual month-end billing and invoicing processes.
