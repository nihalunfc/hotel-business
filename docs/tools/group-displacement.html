How it's calculated

For each night, with capacity C, transient demand D, transient rate ADR and group rooms requested R:

Expected group rooms   g = R × pickup
Rooms held for group   h = g + (R - g) × (1 - resale share)      (never more than C)
Transient without group  = min(D, C)
Transient with group     = min(D, C - h)
Transient displaced      = the difference
Transient profit / room  = ADR × (1 - booking cost %) - variable cost
Group profit / room night = rate × (1 - commission) - variable cost + (food + beverage + meeting) × margin
Net gain                 = group profit - transient profit lost

Break-even group rate is the rate at which net gain is exactly zero across the whole stay:

break-even = (transient profit lost + g_total × (variable cost - extras profit per room night)) / (g_total × (1 - commission))

Verdict. Accept when the group rate is at least break-even plus your safety margin. Negotiate when it is below that but within 10% of break-even (close enough to bridge with rate, dates or a food and beverage minimum). Decline when it is more than 10% below break-even. The counter-offer is break-even plus the safety margin, rounded up to the next $5.

Assumptions and limits. Demand is treated as a single best estimate per night (no uncertainty band), so a cautious safety margin matters most on nights where demand is near capacity. Displaced guests are assumed to be lost, not moved to other nights. Multi-night transient stays that the group breaks up (a guest who can't get Saturday may not book Friday either) are not modelled, so displacement on shoulder nights can be understated. Group rooms that aren't picked up and can't be resold earn nothing; attrition penalties in the contract are not counted. Everything is before tax.
