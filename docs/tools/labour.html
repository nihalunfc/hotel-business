They are rough guides, not authoritative standards: your market, union agreement, brand standards, room size and service level matter more. Use them to spot numbers worth a second look.

How it's calculated

Rooms. Occupied rooms = rooms × occupancy. Stayovers = occupied × stayover share. Check-outs = occupied minus stayovers, and the tool assumes the same number of check-ins (a steady week). Rooms revenue = occupied rooms × ADR.

Housekeeping. Cleaning hours = (check-outs × departure minutes + stayovers × stayover minutes) / 60. Attendant paid hours = cleaning hours × paid hours per shift / productive hours per shift (the rest is breaks, cart set-up and linen runs). Inspectors = attendants (rounded up) / attendants per inspector, rounded up, each working one shift. Turndown hours = occupied rooms × turndown share × turndown minutes / 60.

Front desk. Transaction hours = check-ins / check-ins per agent hour + check-outs / check-outs per agent hour. Because guests arrive in waves, agents spend only part of their time on these, so work hours = transaction hours / share of desk time on check-ins and check-outs.

Food and beverage. Covers (meals served) = occupied rooms × covers per occupied room + outside covers. Server hours = covers / covers per server hour. Kitchen and support hours = server hours × support ratio.

Your own departments. Work hours = units of the chosen driver / units per labour hour + fixed hours per day.

Every department. Minimum hours = minimum people per shift × shifts per day × paid hours per shift. Scheduled hours = the larger of work hours and minimum hours. Shifts = scheduled hours / paid hours per shift, rounded up. FTE = weekly hours / hours in one FTE week. Cost = hours × wage × (1 + benefits load). CPOR = cost / occupied rooms. Labour % = cost / rooms revenue. Weekly figures = daily × days you operate.

Limits. Every day is treated as a typical day; real weeks have Sunday check-out peaks and quiet Tuesdays, so use the staffing guide day by day. Front-office and F&B managers, salaried staff, sales and admin are not included unless you add them. F&B labour is normally judged against F&B revenue, so the rooms-division figure (housekeeping and front desk, plus any department you tick) is shown separately. Nothing you type leaves this browser.
