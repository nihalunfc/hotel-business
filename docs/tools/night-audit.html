How it's calculated

Occupancy = rooms sold ÷ rooms available. By default rooms available is every room in the hotel, because the USALI 11th edition (the Uniform System of Accounts for the Lodging Industry) keeps out-of-order rooms in the count. You can switch to counting only rooms that are not out of order. Complimentary rooms are not in paid occupancy; occupancy including comps is shown on its own line.

ADR (average daily rate) = room revenue ÷ rooms sold (paid rooms only, so comps do not drag it down). RevPAR (revenue per available room) = room revenue ÷ rooms available, which also equals occupancy × ADR. TRevPAR = total revenue ÷ rooms available.

Month and year to date add up the closed nights in History in the same month or year, before tonight's date, plus tonight. Ratios are recalculated from the totals (for example MTD ADR = MTD room revenue ÷ MTD rooms sold), not averaged night by night. Year to date only covers nights saved in this browser.

Over / short = counted − expected, per line. Zero is balanced, within the tolerance is amber and anything larger is red. The total over / short for the night is saved to History.

Rate checks: a $0 rate needs a comp code; a rate below the floor is flagged unless it is an approved discount or comp code; comp and discount codes and direct bill (city ledger) folios need an approval name; a balance above the credit limit is flagged red and one at or above the warning percentage is flagged amber. A cash guest with no credit limit is flagged if they owe anything.

Room status: a room is occupied in the PMS if it is on the in-house list. PMS occupied but housekeeping vacant is a possible sleeper (guest left, folio never checked out) or skip (guest left without paying, likely when the balance is above $0). Housekeeping occupied but PMS vacant may be an unregistered guest or a missed check-in. Rooms missing from either list are listed too.

No-show charge = nightly rate × nights charged by policy × (1 + tax %), only for guaranteed bookings that are not waived. Non-guaranteed bookings are released at no charge.

Limits: this tool checks and records; it does not post to your PMS. Figures you type are trusted, so always run the PMS end-of-day reports too. Everything is saved only in this browser (local storage), so use Download backup to keep a copy. To save space, History stores tonight's checklist, reconciliation, no-shows and report figures, but not the full pasted in-house and housekeeping lists.
