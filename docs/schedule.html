    For BI reviewers: the optimization model
    
The schedule is a mixed-integer program solved with the open-source CBC solver through PuLP (schedule.py). It solves in under a second for 44 employees.

    
    
      
  • Decision variables: one binary per employee, day and allowed shift (AM, MID, short, inspector, evening). Unavailable days have no variables.
    • 
      
  • Hard constraints: one shift per day; at least 11 hours between consecutive shifts (stricter than the 8-hour between-shift rule, and it also guarantees 11 hours off per day); at most 5 shifts for full-time and 4 for part-time, which guarantees 24 consecutive hours off; at most 48 hours in the week.
    • 
      
  • Soft constraints (penalized): uncovered cleaning hours, missing inspectors or evening staff, hours above 44 (overtime at 1.5 times pay), full-time staff below 32 hours, shifts on requested days off, and the maximum number of weekend shifts any full-time employee works (fairness).
    • 
      
  • Objective: minimize wages plus overtime premium plus penalties.
    • 
      
  • Comparison: both schedules are scored with the same evaluator. Uncovered work is costed as call-in hours at 1.5 times pay.
    • 
      
  • Outputs: a colour-coded Excel workbook with live SUM formulas (excel.py), a print layout from this page, and a JSON shift list ready to map to a workforce system's schedule import API (validate first, then post).
