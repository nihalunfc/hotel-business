Staff Schedule Builder

A free tool for building a week's schedule. Enter how busy the hotel will be and who is on your team, then press Build schedule. You get a schedule that covers the work at the lowest cost, respects day-off requests and follows Ontario's hours-of-work rules. You can change any shift by hand and export it to Excel or print it.

How the schedule is built (for BI reviewers)

The builder runs entirely in your browser. It fills the hardest shifts first (evening, then inspector, then room attendants), spreading each day's need across the people who are allowed, rested and under their weekly limits. It picks the lowest-cost person, so it avoids overtime, keeps weekend shifts fair, and only uses someone on a requested day off when nobody else can work. Full-timers are then topped up to their guaranteed hours. A separate checker re-tests every rule on the final schedule, including your hand edits.

The same problem is also solved exactly as a mixed-integer program with PuLP in the analytics pipeline (schedule.py). Its optimised schedules for the three sample weeks are available as Excel workbooks: coming week, peak summer week and quiet winter week.

Your inputs are saved only in this browser (local storage). Nothing is sent anywhere.
